## Editorial rules

Use NASA or ESA primary sources. Cite the specific page for each new fact. Give units and distinguish solar days from rotation periods. Date volatile claims such as moon counts; avoid them where possible. Label compressed distances, exaggerated sizes, and orbit speeds as illustrative when scenes are added. Pluto is a dwarf planet, not one of the eight planets.
