# Asset credits

No external textures, audio or fonts are bundled. The app uses CSS and the device's system font; no font file is distributed. The only external images are the galaxy pictures for the planned Cosmos view, listed below. This is an inventory statement, not a blanket licence for future assets.

## Generated in code (no files)

Planet and Sun surface patterns, ring textures, the Sun's glow and the background stars are drawn at runtime by `src/scenes/proceduralTextures.js` and `src/scenes/solarSystemScene.js`, using colours from `src/scenes/appearance.js`. They are original artist's impressions written for this project (MIT, like the rest of the code). They are not NASA imagery, and the app says so.

## Galaxy images (Cosmos collection)

Prepared by `scripts/galaxies/prepare_images.py` from the originals named in `src/data/galaxies.json` (see docs/COSMOS_DATA.md). Each galaxy has a detail image that fits in 1600 × 1200 px and a thumbnail that fits in 400 × 300 px, both keeping the original's aspect ratio. Nothing else was changed: no cropping, recolouring or retouching. The credit must be shown, unaltered, beside the picture wherever it appears. Tests check that every bundled galaxy image has a row here with its credit.

**Licence notes and open questions**

- **NASA public domain:** the SVS help page and NASA's Hubble exhibit FAQ were read directly. NASA's media usage guidelines (www.nasa.gov), the JPL image use policy and HubbleSite's copyright page could not be opened from the build environment, so they were not re-read for this collection.
- **Joint NASA and ESA credits:** most Hubble images are credited "NASA, ESA, …" and were taken from NASA's pages. Whether ESA's CC BY 4.0 terms also apply to NASA's copies has not been confirmed. Showing the full credit visibly beside each picture meets both NASA's request for credit and ESA's attribution condition.
- **Triangulum Galaxy (heic1901a):** an ESA/Hubble release, CC BY 4.0. The bundled copy was made from NASA's copy of the same image (3000 × 1688). The credit must stay visible and unaltered next to the picture.
- **Magellanic Clouds:** the Swift ultraviolet mosaics are credited "NASA/Swift/S. Immler (Goddard) and M. Siegel (Penn State)". The visible-light pictures on the same NASA page are credited to Axel Mellinger, Central Michigan University, and were deliberately not used.
- **Milky Way (PIA10748):** an artist's concept credited NASA/JPL-Caltech in the NASA Image and Video Library. The JPL Photojournal page, which may give a longer credit line, could not be opened.
- **Acknowledgment lines** (for example "Acknowledgment: P. Knezek (WIYN)") are part of the credit and are kept in full.
