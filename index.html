How to explore
  • Choose a world with the round buttons, or tap it in the picture.
  • With Labels on, tap a planet's name to fly there. A card shows quick facts: More facts opens the full facts, and Hide card tucks it away.
  • Use Back and Next to visit the worlds in order, and Solar system to see them all.
  • Drag the picture to turn it. Pinch, scroll or use + and − to zoom, and ⟲ ⟳ to rotate.
  • Press Pause to stop the planets moving, and press it again to start.
  • With a keyboard: 0 to 8 choose a world, ← and → go back and next, and Esc shows the whole solar system.
